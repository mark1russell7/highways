# The gap closed on `exact-v1`

This note is the result of the item MEAS2 of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md). It ranks each router and each highway strategy of waves 1 to 3 by the gap closed (B10) on the small boards of `exact-v1`.

- The gap closed tells how much of the way from a baseline to the optimum a strategy goes. A value of 0 is the baseline, and a value of 1 is the optimum.
- The optima come from the exact model of fixed-charge network design (HWY-040), solved with HiGHS. The file [`optima-exact-v1.json`](../../packages/core/src/bench/exact/optima-exact-v1.json) keeps them.
- The tables come from the CI run [40617468092](https://github.com/mark1russell7/highways/actions/runs/40617468092) of the workflow `Screen`, at the commit `92d6640`. The run has 8120 records.
- The run did not include the strategies of wave 3D (#93). The screen module `scripts/screens/meas2.ts` adds them at the next run.
- The note [`BOUNDS.md`](BOUNDS.md) gives the gap closed at each cap, and at 18, 24 and 40 nodes (EXACT-BOUNDS).
- The note [`ORACLE.md`](ORACLE.md) gives the best strategy of each input. Its oracle closes a median of 0.947 of the gap.

## Summary

- No strategy closes the gap. The best median is 0.595, from `w3b:growth-s0.3`. Its interval is 0.508 to 0.634.
- The three forms of `growth` lead the table: `w3b:growth-s0.3`, `w3b:growth-input` and `hw:growth`. Their intervals overlap.
- Below `growth`, the leaders are `w3a:spiral-n3` (0.373), `w2b:zipper-p2` (0.372) and `hw:level-links` (0.366). Their intervals overlap from 0.30 to 0.41.
- Many strategies have a median of 0. On most inputs, their B1 is equal to the B1 of the baseline.
- `straight`, `plugs` and four forms of `corners` have a median below 0. They save less ink than the baseline.
- At the cap 1.25 and the rule `collection`, the optimum B1 is 28 to 46 times the B1 of `shared`. Each value is the median of a class.

## The set and the measure

`exact-v1` has 4 classes: `uniform`, `clustered`, `hubs` and `fanout`. Each class has 10 seeds. Each board has 10 nodes and 15 edges. [`LAYOUT-RECORDS.md`](../LAYOUT-RECORDS.md#the-screen-set-exact-v1) gives the generators.

The value of an input is `(B1 - B1 of the baseline) / (the optimum B1 - B1 of the baseline)`.

- The optimum is the optimum at the smallest cap of 1.1, 1.25 and 1.5 that is at or above the largest stretch of the routes.
- The stretch is the stretch of the exact model. It divides the length of a route without its plug ends by the shortest way in the network of the model.
- Above the cap 1.5, a run has no value. When the denominator is 0 or less, a run has no value.
- The rule `collection` lets only the edges of one collection share a link. Its baseline is `shared`.
- The rule `all` lets each edge share a link. Its baseline is `shared-ways-all`, routed with `follow`.
- The chosen rule of a run is `all` when a shared segment of the run holds edges of two collections. Else it is `collection`.
- The median is the median over the 40 inputs. The interval is the 95% paired bootstrap interval of the median, with 10000 samples ([`PROMOTION.md`](../PROMOTION.md)).

The columns of the tables:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `runs` | The runs of the strategy, one for each input. |
| `builds` | The runs with some ink saved (B1 above 0). |
| `mixed` | The runs with a shared segment that holds edges of two collections. |
| `values` | The runs with a value of the gap closed. |
| `median`, `interval` | The median over all the values, and its interval. |
| `clustered`, `fanout`, `hubs`, `uniform` | The median of each class. A `-` is a class without a value. |
| `caps 1.1 / 1.25 / 1.5` | The count of the values at each cap. |

## Notes on the view `collection`
