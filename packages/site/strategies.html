Each card is one strategy of the registry STRATEGIES. The build makes the cards from the registry, so a new strategy gets a card with no change to this page. A card tells the step and the status of the strategy. It shows the ideas that the strategy builds, with their papers, their math and their cost. It also shows the options, the verdicts against the default of the step and the time in the benchmark.
