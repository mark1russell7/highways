A table card of made-up orders, with 6 rows and 4 plugs on its walls. Each reference goes from a row to a plug of the wall inside the card, then from the plug to another table outside the card.

## Try this

- Look at a plug on a wall. The route inside the card and the route outside the card meet at the plug.
- Tap the row **customer id** to show its route to the plug on the wall.
- Zoom in on the card. The routes inside the card go around the other rows.
- Push **Gutters**. Each route inside the card then goes in the gaps between the rows, with a lane for each route.
